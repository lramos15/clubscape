# Release proposal

Status: **draft; no release authorized or accepted**.

The only authorized execution is [M1](milestone-01.md). It is an internal
acceptance milestone, not a declaration of deployable early access or full-game
completion.

The initial proposal is to consider the accepted Section 30 journey for an
explicitly labeled limited-content release only after separate Section 40
approval and verified deployment, TLS, account recovery, operations, load,
backup/restore, security and support criteria. Release capacity and rollout
are not owner-approved yet.

Do not expose incomplete services publicly, publish a release, broaden playable
content, or call this proposal an approval. Missing content stays in the full
contract; all unavailable controls must follow the approved Section 30 policy
once presentation is implemented.
