# First-slice world contract

The required map is the full Tutorial Island route and source-mapped Lumbridge
starter area, including castle, grounds, paths, river/bridge and legitimate
connected acquisition/travel dependencies. Visible scenery remains complete
even where interactions are deliberately limited.

Exact baseline region/tile bounds, spawn/arrival positions, initial cameras,
elevation, collision and object/NPC placements are unresolved at kickoff.
Do not substitute a demonstration square, relocate quest ingredients or
banking, or spawn a pre-completed character in Lumbridge.

Freeze layout inputs and source identities in the
[reference record](reference-baseline.md) and approved presentation pack
before world production. Later declarative region definitions must retain
those source mappings and test travel/access gates.
