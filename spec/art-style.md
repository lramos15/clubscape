# Presentation authority

The first milestone uses the exact stock-visual target and limited adaptations
in `prompt.md` Section 30.2. General penguin-world permissions do not authorize
redesigning ordinary trees, goblins, terrain, buildings, sprites or fonts.

An owner-approved concrete visual/audio reference pack must precede
presentation implementation. It must pin all required source assets,
captures/recordings, camera/render/sampling/font/animation settings, viewport
and resizing range, browser versions, and per-case numeric tolerances.

No such pack is approved at kickoff. No screenshot, generated asset, or
renderer from this execution may be treated as its own source reference.
Missing inputs block presentation acceptance, not independent infrastructure.

See [reference status](reference-baseline.md),
[interface cases](interface-parity.md), [world requirements](world.md) and
[performance prerequisites](performance.md).
