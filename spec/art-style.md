# Presentation authority

The first milestone uses the exact stock-visual target and limited adaptations
in `prompt.md` Section 30.2. General penguin-world permissions do not authorize
redesigning ordinary trees, goblins, terrain, buildings, sprites or fonts.

An owner-approved concrete visual/audio reference pack must precede
presentation implementation. It must pin all required source assets,
captures/recordings, camera/render/sampling/font/animation settings, viewport
and resizing range, browser versions, and per-case numeric tolerances.

Pack v1.3.0 is now owner-approved, recorded in
`milestones/approvals/m1-reference-pack-v1.3.0.json`, against manifest SHA-256
`b62e19704e17d3d3e4e819f803ef49ba7cc54034ae407184b423427c65d9674d`.
Use its exact source images/components, qualified evidence, seven compositions,
penguin/equipment criteria and numeric tolerances. The frozen pack's embedded
`owner_approved: false` fields describe its pre-approval preparation; the
external immutable owner record supplies approval without rewriting its hash.

No ClubScape screenshot may become its own source reference. Final candidate
visual/audio acceptance remains a separate owner gate. Implementation may now
proceed within the approved target, with performance measurements tracked by
`spec/m1-benchmark-contract.json`.
