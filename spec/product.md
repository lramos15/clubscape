# Product

The complete product remains the frozen OSRS gameplay/content/interface
baseline, penguin presentation, and the separately inventoried Club Penguin
layer defined by `prompt.md` Sections 1-5. The full target is not the first
milestone or an early-access release.

See [full scope](full-scope.md) for the unapproved full-contract proposal,
[release scope](launch-scope.md) for release status, and
[M1](milestone-01.md) for the only currently approved execution.

One-to-five-player operation must preserve genuine group requirements and
ordinary progression. No NPC stand-ins, boosted accounts, free acquisition
paths, extra cosmetic equipment layers, or implicit CP reward crossovers are
authorized by the first milestone.
