catalogue, contact sheets, source recording and 258 audio controls are for

## Important source reconciliation

The official October 2025 update supplies actual experience-choice, tutorial
journal, controls-guide, departure and poll images. The current transcript
maps all 71 stages. Those sources do **not** demonstrate every current
stage's complete HUD state. Their former **Tutorial Island** quest title is
not substituted for **Learning the Ropes**. Soft scene outlines documented as
**Official Client only** are not silently added to the selected injected Java
runtime's visuals.

The current native Classic frame has a **241x335** side-panel container,
**211x207** minimap container and **519x165** chat container. The actual 2026
Classic music side-panel crop is traceably reconciled to current source
components. The full RuneLite image, 2015 resizable example and official
music recording instead show **Modern layout**, so they are explicitly
rejected as full Classic-frame baselines. A cropped panel is never called a
full frame. Historical fixed-panel artwork can support only its identified
unchanged components.

February 2026 music-player/slider media and March 2026 level-up media prevent
using the old text-button music UI or 2020 level-up GIF as the current layout.
Current `Audio` documentation identifies the 16-bit SFX update and August 2026
MIDI behavior correction. Original FLAC hashes, decoded PCM, silent effect
probability, frame events and ambient fields remain bound to the existing
audio manifest. The MP4 has actually been decoded, including its audio; that
is **not** a calibrated source mixer, audible product playback or gameplay run.

## Comparison policy and proposed adaptations

[`v1/comparison-policy.json`](v1/comparison-policy.json) sets numeric limits
**before any ClubScape candidate evaluation**. Native glyph masks, advances,
ascent, opaque sprite pixels and palettes permit **zero difference**. Native
scene/model comparisons permit only the narrowly bounded source-edge raster
allowance and exact camera/geometry/frame inputs. JPEG/H.264/GIF allowances
are separate and cannot justify changed controls, fonts, missing scenery or
unidentified camera settings. No whole panels, creatures or scenery are masked.

Primary proposal: **1920x1080 logical pixels, DPR1, UI scale1, 100% browser
zoom, stock Resizable - Classic**. Proposed supported range: both dimensions
between **1024x768 and 2560x1440**, with explicit 1024x768, 1280x800,
1920x1080 and 2560x1440 checkpoints. Static source-anchor arithmetic and
gallery resizing are checked separately; neither proves game resizing.

Seven [`v1/proposals/`](v1/proposals/) images reuse the actual original title
fixture, titlebox/button sprites and native CP1252 glyphs. Their proposed
changes are confined to the declared source titlebox; its frame and every
unchanged source region remain subject to exact comparison. They are labeled
**OWNER-REVIEW PROPOSAL**, not OSRS captures or a new game frontend. The
source logo is deliberately retained for attribution; the pack does not
fabricate or approve final ClubScape logo artwork.

Original Penguin NPC2063 at 75/128 source scale is an unapproved reuse-first
player candidate. Its complete native idle/walk frames are provided.
Equipment attachment/fitting, all functional slots, branding and web-only
wording receive separate bounded owner review. They do not relax ordinary
tree, goblin, terrain, building or source-interface requirements.
